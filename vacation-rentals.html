For Rental Hosts

Chimney care for cabins and vacation rentals

Guests love a fire. You need it to be safe. We keep fireplaces and stoves in Shenandoah Valley and Blue Ridge rentals clean, inspected and documented, on a schedule that works around your bookings.

Why Rentals Are Different

A rental fireplace works harder than a home fireplace

Every week brings new guests who do not know your fireplace or stove. That changes how fast it wears and what can go wrong between visits.

  • Guests burn differently. Bigger fires, damp wood and doors left open build creosote faster than everyday use.
  • Empty weeks invite trouble. Birds, squirrels and rain find their way into uncapped flues while the cabin sits empty.
  • Nobody is there to notice. A smoky room or a strange smell may go unreported until it shows up in a review.
  • Records matter. A dated inspection shows you are keeping up with the fireplace, which insurers and property managers may ask about.

For Your House Manual

Fireplace tips for your guests

Feel free to copy these into your welcome book or listing. Clear rules up front prevent most of the problems we see in rentals.

Hosts: test the smoke and carbon monoxide alarms at every turnover.

Using the fireplace

  1. Open the damper before lighting a fire, and leave it open until the fire is completely out.
  2. Burn only the dry firewood we provide. No trash, cardboard, wrapping paper or painted wood.
  3. Keep fires small to medium, and never use lighter fluid or gasoline.
  4. Keep the screen or glass doors closed, and keep rugs, blankets and furniture at least 3 feet away.
  5. Never leave a fire unattended or go to bed while it is still burning.
  6. Put ashes only in the lidded metal container provided, and set it outside, away from the house.
  7. If smoke comes into the room or you smell something unusual, let the fire burn down and contact us.

Do I need to be at the property?

No. Many hosts send us the access details, and we text photos and a summary when we are done.

How often should a rental chimney be swept?

At least once a year, before the burning season. Rentals with heavy winter bookings often need a second sweep partway through the season, and we can check the buildup and tell you.

Can you work around my bookings?

Yes. Tell us your checkout and check-in times and we will schedule the visit between guests, Monday through Saturday.

Do you provide a record of the work?

Yes. You get dated photos and a plain-language summary of what we cleaned, inspected and found, to keep with your property records.
