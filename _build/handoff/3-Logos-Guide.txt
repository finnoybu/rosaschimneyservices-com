ROSAS CHIMNEY SERVICES LOGOS
=============================================================================
Every logo comes in two formats:
  .svg  Sharp at any size. Give this one to printers, sign and vehicle
        shops, and embroidery or t-shirt companies.
  .png  A ready-made image with a see-through background, for documents,
        email signatures and social media.

WHICH ONE TO USE
  rosas-logo-primary            The main shield logo. Light backgrounds.
  rosas-logo-primary-cream      The shield filled with cream. Works on ANY
                                background, light or dark. Used in the
                                website footer.
  rosas-logo-primary-reversed   Shield with cream lettering, for dark
                                backgrounds (dark shirts, dark vehicles).
  rosas-logo-horizontal         Wide version for letterhead, invoices,
                                email signatures, light backgrounds.
  rosas-logo-horizontal-reversed  Wide version for dark backgrounds. Used in
                                the website header.
  rosas-logo-icon               Shield and emblem, no words. Social media
                                profile pictures, stickers, small spaces.
  rosas-logo-icon-reversed      Same, for dark backgrounds.
  rosas-favicon.svg             The tiny browser-tab icon.

COLORS (for printers)
  Navy   #2B3D4F        Brick red  #A63C24
  Gold   #D79136        Cream      #F6EBDD
